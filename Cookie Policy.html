What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you use our platform, and improve your experience.

At DARAWAT, we use cookies to make our platform work better for you. They help us remember your login status, language preferences, and shopping cart items.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies
These cookies are necessary for the platform to function properly. They enable core features like authentication and security.
Always Active

Optional
Functional Cookies
These cookies remember your preferences and settings, like language and region.
Optional

These cookies help us show relevant content and offers based on your interests.
Optional

How We Use Cookies

Authentication: We use cookies to keep you logged in and maintain your session.

Preferences: Cookies remember your language, theme, and other personal settings.

Analytics: We use analytics cookies to understand how you use FarmNet and improve our platform.

Security: Cookies help us protect your account and prevent fraud.

How Long Do Cookies Last?

Session Cookies: These cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser.

Persistent Cookies: These cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

We use both types to provide you with the best experience on FarmNet.
