Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about DARAWAT

🧑‍🌾

For Farmers & Sellers

How do I list my products?

Just sign up, choose "Seller" as your role, and tap "Add Product." Upload a photo, set your price, and choose availability dates. You can write descriptions in Tagalog, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, or Waray.

Can I use FarmNet without internet?

Yes! FarmNet is offline-ready. You can manage inventory, view orders, and update listings even in low-signal areas. Changes sync automatically when you reconnect.

What if I don't have a smartphone?

You can access FarmNet via desktop or shared devices at cooperatives or barangay hubs. SMS support is also available for basic functions.

🛒

For Buyers

How do I place an order?

Browse products, add items to your cart, choose delivery or pickup, and confirm payment. You'll get real-time updates and seller messages.

What payment methods are accepted?

FarmNet supports mobile wallets (GCash, Maya), cash-on-delivery, and cooperative credits. More options are coming soon.

Can I trust the sellers?

Yes. Each seller has a profile, trust rating, and farm story. You can chat with them directly and leave reviews after purchase.

🧩

Platform Features

What languages does FarmNet support?

Tagalog, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Waray — with more coming soon. You can switch languages anytime in the settings.

What makes FarmNet different from other marketplaces?

FarmNet is built for Filipino agriculture. It's modular, offline-first, emoji-enhanced, and designed with local pride. We support farmers, cooperatives, and LGUs — not just transactions.

Is my data safe?

Yes. FarmNet uses encrypted transactions, secure logins, and consent-based data sharing. We never sell your information.

🤝

Partnerships & Support

Can LGUs or NGOs use FarmNet?

Absolutely. We offer admin dashboards, training modules, and onboarding support for government and nonprofit partners.

How do I get help?

Tap the 🧑‍🌾 icon in the app, email support@farmnet.ph, or text "TANONG" to 2929. We respond in your preferred language.

Still Need Help?

Our support team is ready to assist you in Tagalog, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, or Waray.
