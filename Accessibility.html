Accessibility

DARAWAT is committed to making our platform accessible to everyone

Our Commitment

DARAWAT is dedicated to ensuring that our platform is accessible to all users, regardless of ability or disability. We believe that everyone should have equal access to fresh, locally-sourced products.

Accessibility Features

DARAWAT includes a range of features designed to make the platform usable for everyone. We follow WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.1 AA standards.

Keyboard Navigation
All features can be accessed using keyboard shortcuts, making navigation easy without a mouse.
Readable Fonts
We use clear, readable fonts and proper contrast ratios for easy reading.
Responsive Design
FarmNet works on all devices and screen sizes, including mobile phones and tablets.
Screen Reader Compatible
Our platform is compatible with screen readers and other assistive technologies.
Multi-Language Support
FarmNet supports multiple languages including Tagalog, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, and Waray.
Alt Text for Images
All images include descriptive alt text for screen readers and users with visual impairments.
Color Contrast
High contrast colors are used throughout the platform for better visibility.
No Time Limits
Users can take their time with actions and forms without time pressure.

WCAG Compliance

FarmNet follows the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level. This means our platform is:

Perceivable: Information and user interface components are presented in ways that all users can perceive.

Operable: User interface components and navigation are operable by everyone.

Understandable: Information and operation of user interface are understandable.

Robust: Content can be interpreted reliably by a wide variety of user agents, including assistive technologies.

Ongoing Efforts

We continuously work to improve the accessibility of FarmNet. Our efforts include:

Regular Audits: We regularly test our platform for accessibility issues and address them promptly.

User Feedback: We actively seek and incorporate feedback from users with disabilities.

Staff Training: Our team is trained on accessibility best practices and inclusive design.

Continuous Improvement: We're committed to making FarmNet more accessible with every update.

Accessibility Feedback

We value your feedback! If you encounter any accessibility barriers on FarmNet, please let us know. Your input helps us improve.

Email: accessibility@farmnet.ph

Phone: 1-800-FARMNET

We aim to respond to accessibility concerns within 48 hours.
