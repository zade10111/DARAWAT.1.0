How DARAWAT Works

DARAWAT makes farm-to-market simple, inclusive, and joyful. Here's how it all comes together:

🧑‍🌾

1. Sign Up & Set Your Role

Choose your path and get started in your preferred language.

Farmer/Seller: List your harvest, set prices, and manage orders
Buyer: Browse fresh produce, place orders, and track deliveries
Cooperative/Admin: Oversee multiple sellers and manage logistics
Language Options: Tagalog, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Waray
📸

2. List Your Products

Showcase your farm products with rich details and visuals.

Photos (farm-fresh or emoji-enhanced 🌽🍅)
Descriptions in your preferred language
Pricing (per kilo, bundle, or bulk)
Availability (harvest windows, delivery days)
🛒

3. Browse & Buy

Find fresh produce and connect directly with farmers.

Filter by location, product type, or seller rating
Real-time updates on availability
Pay via mobile wallet, cash-on-delivery, or credits
View farm stories and seller profiles

4. Arrange Delivery

Choose the delivery method that works best for you.

Pickup: Meet at the farm or barangay hub
Drop-off: Barangay delivery or third-party courier
Bulk logistics: For cooperatives and LGUs
Smart routing: Efficient deliveries even in rural areas
📊

5. Track & Manage

Stay on top of your business with powerful tools.

Monitor inventory and sales in real-time
Export summaries for accounting or LGU reporting
Get alerts for low stock, new orders, or delivery updates
Offline-first design works without internet signal
💬

6. Connect & Collaborate

Join a thriving agricultural community.

In-app messaging for buyers and sellers
Community forums for tips and farmer stories
Support channels in multiple languages
Partner tools for LGUs, NGOs, and cooperatives
🌱

7. Grow With Us

Unlock new opportunities as you use FarmNet.

Seller badges and trust ratings
Access to training modules and agri-tech tips
Invitations to regional showcases and farmer spotlights
Early access to crop forecasting and smart tagging
