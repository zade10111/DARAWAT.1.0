Our Story

Born from the soil of Filipino ingenuity and nurtured by the spirit of collaboration

DARAWAT is more than a platform — it's a movement. Born from the soil of Filipino ingenuity and nurtured by the spirit of collaboration, FarmNet connects farmers, buyers, and communities through a digital experience that's both joyful and powerful.

We believe that technology should feel like home. That's why DARAWAT is designed with playful authenticity, regional pride, and pixel-perfect care. From emoji-powered tractor smoke trails 🚜💨 to modular dashboards that adapt to your needs, every detail reflects our commitment to accessibility, inclusivity, and local relevance.

Whether you're a farmer in Bukidnon, a buyer in Cebu, or a barangay cooperative in Leyte, FarmNet brings you closer to the people and products that matter most.

🌐 Language & Accessibility

FarmNet proudly supports Visayan language options and inclusive design

Regional Language Support

DARAWAT proudly supports Visayan language options, including Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Waray, and more. We believe that regional language support isn't just a feature — it's a statement of respect.

Accessibility Features

  • Offline-first architecture using service workers
  • Responsive design for all devices
  • Clear iconography and intuitive navigation
