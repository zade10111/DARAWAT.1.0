Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you use FarmNet, we may collect:

Personal Information

  • Name, contact details, location
  • Language preference
  • Account credentials

Usage Data

  • Pages visited and features used
  • Device type and browser information
  • Platform performance metrics

Transaction Data

  • Orders, payments, and delivery details
  • Seller and buyer interactions
  • Payment method preferences

Optional Data

  • Farm photos and product descriptions
  • Feedback and reviews
  • Communication preferences

We only collect what's needed to improve your experience and support platform features.

🧠

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve FarmNet services:

  • Create and manage your account - Personalize your experience based on your role and preferences
  • Process orders and payments - Facilitate secure transactions between buyers and sellers
  • Improve platform performance - Optimize features for different devices and connection types
  • Provide customer support - Assist you in your preferred language
  • Send updates and alerts - Keep you informed about orders, deliveries, and platform news

We do not sell your data. Ever. Your trust is the foundation of our platform.

🌐

Language & Accessibility

Your language preference helps us deliver content you understand and feel comfortable with.

  • We support Tagalog, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Waray, and English
  • Your language setting is stored securely
  • We use this information only to improve your experience
  • You can change your language preference anytime in settings

Our accessibility features ensure everyone can use FarmNet, regardless of device, literacy level, or internet connectivity.

🔒

Data Security

FarmNet uses multiple layers of security to protect your information:

Encryption & Protection

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data transmission
  • Secure login and password protection
  • Regular security audits and updates

Access Control

  • Role-based access for admins and cooperatives
  • Limited data access based on user roles
  • Regular review of access permissions

Offline Security

  • Offline-first design with local storage protections
  • Secure data synchronization when reconnecting
  • Local data encryption on your device

We regularly audit our systems to prevent unauthorized access or data loss.

🤝

Sharing & Consent

We believe in transparent data sharing with your consent:

When We Share Data

  • Delivery partners - Only information needed for logistics and delivery
  • LGUs or NGOs - Only with your explicit consent, for training or support programs
  • Technical providers - To maintain and improve platform features

Your Control

  • You control what information is shared
  • You can update permissions anytime in your settings
  • We never share sensitive personal information without explicit consent
  • You can withdraw consent at any time

You're in control - Manage your privacy settings in the app or web dashboard.

🧑‍🌾

Your Rights

You have full control over your personal information:

  • View and edit your profile data anytime
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Change language or notification settings to match your preferences
  • Ask us what data we've stored about you
  • Export your data for personal records
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, simply tap "Privacy Settings" in the app or email us at privacy@farmnet.ph.

📣

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect:

  • New platform features or services
  • Changes in legal requirements
  • User feedback and community needs
  • Technological advancements

When we make significant changes:

  • We'll notify you via email or in-app alerts
  • We'll provide a summary of what's changing
  • We'll give you time to review before changes take effect
  • We'll always maintain the core privacy principles

Continued use of FarmNet after updates means you accept the revised policy.
