Community Guidelines

Our commitment to building a safe, respectful, and thriving agricultural community

🌱 Welcome to the DARAWAT community! We're building a platform where farmers, buyers, and partners can connect, grow, and succeed together. These guidelines help us maintain a positive environment for everyone. Please take a moment to read and understand them.

Be Respectful and Kind
Treat every member of the FarmNet community with respect and kindness. Whether you're a farmer, buyer, or partner, everyone deserves to be treated with dignity.
  • Use polite and professional language
  • Respect different opinions and backgrounds
  • No harassment, bullying, or discriminatory behavior
  • Be patient and understanding with others
Be Honest and Transparent
Trust is the foundation of our community. Always be truthful and transparent in your interactions.
  • Provide accurate product descriptions and pricing
  • Honor your commitments and promises
  • Don't misrepresent yourself or your products
  • Report issues honestly and constructively
Stay Safe and Secure
Your safety and security matter to us. Take steps to protect yourself and others on the platform.
  • Don't share personal contact information publicly
  • Report suspicious activity immediately
  • Use secure payment methods
  • Verify seller and product information before transactions
Support Each Other
DARAWAT is built on community support. Help others succeed and grow together.
  • Share knowledge and farming tips
  • Offer constructive feedback
  • Celebrate others' successes
  • Help new members learn the platform
Follow Platform Rules
Adhere to DARAWAT's policies and terms of service to maintain a fair and orderly marketplace.
  • Don't engage in fraudulent activities
  • Don't spam or promote unrelated content
  • Respect intellectual property rights
  • Follow all applicable laws and regulations
Be a Positive Contributor
Your actions shape the community. Be the kind of member you'd want to interact with.
  • Share your success stories
  • Provide helpful reviews and feedback
  • Recommend good sellers to others
  • Be an active and engaged community member
